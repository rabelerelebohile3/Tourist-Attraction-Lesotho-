About Lesotho

The Mountain Kingdom

Lesotho is a country in Southern Africa totally surrounded by South Africa.

It is known for its high mountains, beautiful scenery and Basotho culture.

The people of Lesotho are called Basotho. Sesotho and English are the official languages.

Some facts about Lesotho

Capital city Maseru
People Basotho
Currency Loti (Maloti)
Languages Sesotho and English
